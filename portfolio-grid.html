portfolio grid

cat context.txt

the layout system behind carriemarkusen.com, built on the golden ratio.

rows sit at 1/φ³, 1/φ² and 1/φ of the screen’s height, and the main columns split its width by the same proportions. lines and type are both a share of the window, so everything scales together.

./baseline-areas

the baseline areas come from subdividing a golden rectangle.

open .com-grid-map

layout and type specifications are set to the final grid. rows and outer margins follow the screen’s height, so the margins stay square; inner columns follow its width.
